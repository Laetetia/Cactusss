Vestige

We were so young when we found each other. Still becoming, still soft around the edges, still figuring out who we were going to be, two young hearts writing forever in pencil; soft, hopeful, erasable. You held my hand like it was a promise, and I believed you the way morning believes it will always return. Four years, stitched together with love and hope that felt eternal, with plans that stretched farther than our understanding of distance, farther than patience could carry. We said always like it was light, like it cost nothing to hold. You expressed your fears, fears that reflected my own. You promised to always wait for me, promised not to leave me, and I nodded nodded with my heart too. But distance, distance is a quiet thief. It doesn’t shout, it doesn’t fight, it just loosens the threads until love hangs by memory alone. And you, you became quieter before you became gone. Et peu importe ce que c’était ; du rejet ou autre chose de plus silencieux.. De ta part, ce fut la cerise sur le gâteau, car je faisais déjà face à bien des pertes silencieuses. Ça, c’est ce qui m’a fait le plus mal. Je ne te pensais pas capable d’être aussi froid, je n’ai jamais compris comment mon garçon si doux avant a pu me tourner le dos comme ça and me? I filled the silence with questions, with prayers, with pieces of myself I thought might reach you if I broke them small enough. But silence, silence has a language I was too in love to translate. It said: you are no longer chosen. I am tired of trying. there is somewhere else I breathe easier. And still, I spoke louder, held in my heart the stubborn hope of love, as if love could outshout absence. I thought we were the kind of love that always finds its way back. Because we always did, didn’t we? Until we didn’t. Until your absence stayed. Until my name no longer lived in your days. I think what broke me was not the leaving, but how quietly you made it feel like I was easy to leave. Your silence, despite my desperate cries, despite my pleas was a fracture that spread through my soul. It crept in through the spaces between messages, between calls that never came, between the days I watched my phone and felt myself grow smaller. That silence said: you were easy to let go of. you did not interrupt his peace. your cries were not the kind that make someone stay. And that, that is what broke the heart. Not that it ended, but the ease of it. Like all our years could fold into nothing without a sound. Now I sit with a love that has nowhere to go, a future that still wears your face, and a heart that doesn’t know how to unlearn you. I was not a perfect lover. I broke promises too. I failed you in ways I am still accounting for. I am not writing this to make you a villain. I am writing this to make amends with myself, to be at peace with myself, and to finally let the weight fall where it belongs. Memories I leave I do not leave with a heavy heart, but rather a lighter one. With lessons I have learned towards the end, and a promise to myself: I shall never demean myself in the presence of any other man’s silence ever again. I am writing this because it happened, and it deserves to be witnessed. Four years deserve more than silence. We were young, that’s what they’ll say. But we were real. And maybe that’s why it hurts this way. because something true ended without ever learning how to last.

Adieu, toi que je porterai toujours dans, mon cœur
